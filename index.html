FROM DRAFT TO DOWNLOAD

A home for your words.

  1. Bring your manuscript. Import a .docx or UTF-8 .txt file, or paste your text. Heading 1 in Word and lines like “Chapter 1” become chapters.
  2. Make it yours. Edit chapters, add your title and author, and include optional front and back matter.
  3. Preview and export. Pick a style and download your EPUB or full package. Check the final files in your preferred reader before publishing.

Files are processed on your device. A local draft is saved in this browser when storage is available. Save a project backup to keep a portable copy.

Best for text-focused books. Complex Word layouts are simplified; headers, footers, floating shapes and print pagination are not carried over.

Replace this manuscript?

Your current chapters will be replaced. Download a project backup first if you need a copy.
